Company Background

Engineering Packaging Excellence Since 2005

Established in 2005, Adeel Engineering Works is a specialized industrial enterprise manufacturing automatic and semi-automatic packing machines for granules, powders, and liquids.

Under the leadership of CEO Adeel Ahmed Niazi, we deliver robust machinery built with heavy-gauge 12mm 304/316 stainless steel, Siemens and Delta PLC automation, high-sensitivity photocells, and German-sourced critical parts. We also engineer irregular and custom packing machines according to unique customer specifications.

Engineering Competencies

Industrial Capabilities Built for Heavy Factory Duty

From individual dosing units to end-to-end turnkey lines, every machine is built with precision mechanics and industrial-grade automation.

PLC & HMI Automation

Siemens and Delta programmable controllers paired with intuitive 7-inch color touchscreens for non-stop parameter adjustments and digital temperature control.

Servo Motion & Registration

High-torque servo drives and high-sensitivity photoelectric sensors (photocells) for straight, jitter-free cutting aligned precisely with printed film eye-marks.

German Component Integration

Critical high-wear parts such as bearings, pneumatic valves, motors, and main lifting jacks are sourced from premier German manufacturers for lasting durability.

Customized Machinery

Full engineering consultation to design and fabricate irregular packing machines, custom forming collars, and variable sachet shapes (twin, contour, pillow).

Anti-Waste Interlocks

Intelligent optical fault sensing stops packaging instantly if the material hopper empties or the film roll runs out, preventing wrapper waste and downtime.

Turnkey Processing Lines

Complete packaging ecosystems including screw auger feeders, Z-bucket conveyors, ribbon mixers, hot air dryers, coating pans, and band sealing equipment.
